Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026
Developer: Cycloid
Contact: chengyuchunrd@7fenzhong.com

StarryBalloon (“the App”) is a local altimeter, compass, and track-recording tool. This policy explains how the App accesses, stores, and uses information.

1. Information we access

The App does not create accounts, does not require sign-in, and does not upload your personal data to our servers. The App currently does not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party data services.

To provide core features, the App accesses the following on your device:

  • Precise location (GPS): latitude, longitude, altitude, speed, heading, accuracy, and timestamps — used to show altitude, coordinates, and speed, and to record tracks.
  • Motion / environment sensors: barometer (pressure and relative altitude) and magnetometer (compass heading), used only for on-device instrument display.
  • Tracks you choose to record: name, start/end time, track points (location, altitude, speed, heading), and derived stats such as distance and elevation gain.
  • App settings: language, units, coordinate format, sampling rate, keep-screen-on, and similar preferences.

Location may be used while the App is in the foreground. If you grant Always / background location permission, the App may also use location in the background to keep recording a track. You can revoke location access in iOS Settings at any time; related features will then stop working.

2. Storage and use

  • Location, tracks, and settings are stored only on your device (local database and local storage).
  • They are used solely to display live instruments, compute on-device estimates (such as sunrise/sunset), and record / review tracks.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Sharing

We do not transmit your location, tracks, or other personal data to ourselves or to third parties. Deleting a track in the App, clearing app data, or uninstalling the App removes the corresponding local data (device backups may still contain copies depending on your backup settings).

4. Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete a track, clear app data, or uninstall the App. You can delete saved tracks on the Tracks screen.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Third-party services

This version does not integrate analytics, ads, or cloud sync. If we add such services later, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy nutrition labels.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of the App means you are aware of the updated policy.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email chengyuchunrd@7fenzhong.com.

如需帮助（定位权限、轨迹录制、单位设置、审核测试等），请发送邮件至 chengyuchunrd@7fenzhong.com，并尽量附上设备型号与 iOS 版本。

English

Thank you for using StarryBalloon, a local altimeter, compass, and track recorder. No account is required.

For help with permissions, track recording, units, or App Review testing, email chengyuchunrd@7fenzhong.com and include your device model and iOS version if possible.

  • Location access is required for altitude, speed, and track recording.
  • Tracks stay on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Continued use of GPS in the background can significantly decrease battery life.
